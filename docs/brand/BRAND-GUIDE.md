# ORYVA by Sun — Brand Guide v1.0

**Product:** SUN-001 · **Tagline:** Identity. Trust. Protection.

## Approved direction

The original sunrise and mountain identity is the sole ORYVA brand direction. The sun arc connects ORYVA to SUN; mountain peaks convey resilience and trust; the four-point star conveys clarity. Do not substitute shield, keyhole or hexagonal logos.

## Usage

Use the panoramic asset above the README title. Preserve the tagline and `by Sun` attribution. Keep sufficient clear space around the icon and do not stretch artwork or recolor the sun arc. The icon is suitable for product UI at larger sizes; test legibility at favicon sizes before adopting it there.
